Questions

Is Paisa Pulse really free?

Yes. Everything listed here is free, with no ads and no limits. A Pro plan for shared extras will come later, and it will be optional.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Backups go to iCloud Drive or your phone's storage, encrypted with your password if you set one.

Does it read all my messages?

Only messages that look like a debit or credit are kept, and only on your phone. OTPs, payment requests and due-date reminders are ignored. On iPhone, you choose which messages to pass along with a Shortcuts automation.

Which banks and apps does it understand?

Bank SMS from Axis, Bank of Baroda, HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Kotak and most other Indian banks; screenshots from Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED and bank apps; and statements from Axis, Bank of Baroda, HDFC, ICICI and SBI. Other statements work through a column-matching step.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Reading messages, screenshots and statements all happens on the phone. Internet is only used for optional things: currency rates, merchant logos and splits shared with friends.

How do I get it on iPhone?

The iPhone app is in early access and installed outside the App Store for now. The download page has the steps; an App Store version is planned.

Can I move between Android and iPhone?

Yes. Both apps read and write the same backup file, so your data moves with you.
