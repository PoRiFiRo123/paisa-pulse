Account deletion

Delete your account

You can delete your Paisa Pulse account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Open Paisa Pulse › Settings › Account › Delete Account, then confirm. It happens straight away.

Without the app

Email hello@nishitrk.tech from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in (email and password), name, @username and phone fingerprint.
  • Your connections with friends, and the split expenses, settle-ups, groups and trips you shared. Friends keep their own copies of amounts they recorded on their phones.

Nothing is kept after deletion. Your personal ledger was never on our servers: it stays on your phone until you delete it there (Settings › Clean Up Data › Delete All Data) or uninstall the app.
