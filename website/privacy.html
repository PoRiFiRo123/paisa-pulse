Privacy Policy

Short version: your money data stays on your phone. We don't run ads, don't track you, and don't sell anything about you. An account is optional and only used for things you choose to share with friends.

Who we are

Paisa Pulse is a personal finance app for Android and iPhone made by Nishit Kirani in Bengaluru, India. Questions about this policy: hello@nishitrk.tech.

Data that stays on your phone

Everything you record — accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, bills, loans, trips, cards, tags, notes and receipt photos — is stored only on your device. We have no server holding your ledger and we can't see it.

  • Bank messages and notifications (auto-capture). If you turn it on, the app reads incoming bank SMS (Android, outside Google Play), payment notifications (Android), or messages you pass to it with a Shortcuts automation (iPhone). Only messages that look like a debit or credit are kept, as drafts for you to confirm. OTPs, payment requests and due reminders are ignored. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Screenshots and statements. Text is read with your phone's own text recognition (Apple Vision or Google ML Kit on the device). Images and files are not uploaded.
  • Cards. When you add a card we keep only the network, last 4 digits, expiry, name and colour. The full card number and CVV are never stored.
  • Backups. Backups are files on your phone, in iCloud Drive or in Google's Android backup, under your own account. If you set a backup password they are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Sign-in tokens are excluded from Android backups.

If you create an account (optional)

An account is only needed to split expenses and trips with friends who also use Paisa Pulse. It's provided through Supabase, our hosting provider. We keep:

  • your email address and password (the password is stored hashed by Supabase, never in readable form);
  • the name and @username you choose;
  • if you add it, a fingerprint of your phone number (a SHA-256 hash of the last 10 digits), so friends can find you;
  • the people you connect with, and the split expenses, settle-ups, groups and trips you share with them. These are visible to the people you share them with.

Finding friends from contacts. If you allow it, the app turns each phone number in your contacts into the same kind of fingerprint on your phone and sends only the fingerprints to find matches. Names and numbers never leave your phone, and the fingerprints are used for the match and not stored.

Emails. We send email only when you ask to reset your password (a 6-digit code), through our email provider Resend.

Other services the app talks to

  • Exchange rates: open.er-api.com, to convert currencies. No personal data is sent.
  • Merchant logos (off by default): if you turn it on, the app asks Google's favicon service for a shop's logo using only the shop's website name (for example swiggy.com).
  • Updates (GitHub builds only): the app checks GitHub for a newer version. No personal data is sent.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we don't sell or share your data with data brokers or advertisers.

Permissions

Each permission is asked for only when you use the feature that needs it, and the app keeps working if you say no: camera (scan a card), photos (pick screenshots), contacts (find friends), notifications (reminders, and reading payment alerts on Android), SMS (Android builds outside Google Play), Face ID or fingerprint (app lock).

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings › Account › Delete Account. This removes your profile, connections and the items you shared from our servers.
  • Delete your data on the phone: Settings › Clean Up Data › Delete All Data, or uninstall the app.
  • Get a copy: export your transactions as CSV or a backup file at any time. For a copy of your account data, email us.
  • You can withdraw any permission in your phone's settings.

You can contact us about accessing, correcting or deleting your personal data, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, at hello@nishitrk.tech. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. On the server, your data can only be reached through functions that check who you are. On the phone, you can turn on Face ID or fingerprint lock and hide amounts with one tap.

Children

Paisa Pulse is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
