iPhone

Early access, iOS 18 or newer. An App Store version is planned. For now the app is installed with your own free Apple ID:

  1. Download PaisaPulse.ipa from the latest release.
  2. Install it with a sideloading tool such as SideStore or AltStore, signed in with your Apple ID.
  3. On the iPhone, open Settings › General › VPN & Device Management and trust your Apple ID.

With a free Apple ID, apps need re-signing every 7 days; SideStore does it for you.
